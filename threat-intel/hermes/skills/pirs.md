# Priority Intelligence Requirements (homelab, financial sector focus)

Edit this file to change what the briefs prioritise. Briefs group findings by PIR.

PIR-1 Financial sector targeting
  Which actors are targeting banks, payment processors, brokerages, insurers or fintech,
  and with what initial access (phishing, IAB access sales, exposed services, supply chain)?

PIR-2 Ransomware and extortion
  Which ransomware or extortion groups are active this week, which new victims are in
  financial services, and what TTPs or vulnerabilities did they use?

PIR-3 Tracked actors
  New reporting on FIN7 / Carbanak, Lazarus Group (and DPRK clusters), TA505,
  Volt Typhoon, Scattered Spider, and any actor newly linked to the financial sector.

PIR-4 Exploited vulnerabilities
  New CISA KEV entries, high-EPSS CVEs, and actively exploited flaws in internet-facing
  products common in large enterprises (VPNs, firewalls, MFT, identity, Citrix, Ivanti,
  Fortinet, Palo Alto, Microsoft Exchange/SharePoint, Okta).

PIR-5 Fraud and credential exposure
  Infostealer activity, credential and session-token theft, card fraud, BEC, and
  account-takeover tradecraft relevant to banks (MITRE F3 Fight Fraud Framework).

PIR-6 AI and emerging tech threats
  Attacks on or using AI systems (MITRE ATLAS), prompt injection, model or AI supply
  chain abuse, and AI-enabled fraud.
